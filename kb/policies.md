Refund policy: Failed or duplicate payments are refunded to the original payment method within 5-7 working days. Ask the customer to share the transaction reference only if the refund is delayed beyond 7 days. Priority: high.

Delivery policy: Delayed orders are tracked by the logistics team; customers get a revised delivery date within 24 hours. Orders delayed over 10 days are eligible for cancellation with full refund. Priority: medium.

Account access: Locked accounts are unlocked via the "Forgot password" link. Suspected unauthorized access must be escalated immediately to the security team and the account frozen. Priority: critical.

Service quality: Complaints about staff behaviour are logged and reviewed by a supervisor within 48 hours; the customer receives an apology and outcome update. Priority: low.

Wrong or damaged item: Customers receiving a wrong or damaged item get a free replacement or full refund; pickup is scheduled within 48 hours. Photos are requested only if the claim is disputed. Priority: medium.

Billing errors: Overcharges and incorrect invoices are corrected within 3 working days and the difference credited to the account. Priority: medium.

Order cancellation policy: Customers can cancel orders before they are dispatched. Once an order has been shipped, cancellation requests must be handled through the return process. Eligible cancellations are refunded to the original payment method within 5-7 working days. Priority: medium.

Payment pending policy: If a payment remains pending but the customer has been charged, the billing team verifies the transaction with the payment gateway. If the payment is unsuccessful, the amount is refunded within 5-7 working days. Customers should not make another payment until the previous transaction is verified. Priority: high.

Missing order policy: If an order is marked as delivered but the customer has not received it, the logistics team investigates the delivery confirmation and proof of delivery. The investigation must be initiated within 24 hours. Priority: high.

Incorrect delivery address policy: Customers requesting an address change must contact support before the order is dispatched. Address changes after dispatch depend on logistics partner approval. If the change cannot be completed, the customer may request cancellation after delivery according to the return policy. Priority: medium.

Return and exchange policy: Customers can request a return or exchange within 7 days of receiving an eligible product. The returned product must meet the applicable return conditions. Pickup is arranged after return approval, and eligible refunds are processed within 5-7 working days. Priority: medium.

Account suspension policy: Customers whose accounts are suspended must contact support for verification and clarification. The support team reviews the suspension reason and forwards incorrect suspension cases to the account administration team. Priority: medium.

Account deletion policy: Customers can request permanent account deletion through the official support channel. The account team verifies ownership and processes eligible deletion requests according to applicable data retention requirements. Priority: low.

Application error policy: Complaints involving application crashes, login failures, or unexpected error messages must be recorded with the affected feature and error details. The technical support team investigates the issue and provides an update within 24 hours. Priority: medium.

Service outage policy: When multiple customers report that the same service is unavailable, the technical operations team investigates the system immediately. Major production outages must be escalated to the infrastructure team, and customers receive updates until service is restored. Priority: critical.

Unresolved complaint policy: If a customer reports that a previously submitted grievance remains unresolved beyond the committed resolution time, the complaint must be escalated to the department supervisor. The supervisor reviews the complaint history and provides an updated resolution plan within 24 hours. Priority: high.
